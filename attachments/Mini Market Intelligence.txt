Mini Market Intelligence

A small, interactive business intelligence web app using a public/synthetic dataset.

The idea:

"Explore where products, customers and revenue are performing — and identify opportunities."

It would look like a compact version of a real management analytics system.

Make it interactive

The user can filter:

Year

2024 | 2025 | 2026

Region

All | Western | Central | Southern | Northern

Category

All | Electronics | Home | Lifestyle | ...

The entire dashboard updates.

Add actual business intelligence

Instead of only showing charts, have an Insights section.

For example:

Market signal

Western region contributes 41% of revenue but has declining growth over the last 3 months.

Product opportunity

Category X has 18% revenue growth while representing only 7% of total sales.

Customer signal

Repeat customers generate 2.4× higher average order value than one-time customers.

Risk

Five products account for 46% of category revenue, indicating concentration risk.

These can be calculated dynamically from the dataset.

That makes the project much closer to your actual analytics work without exposing any LankaTiles data.

One feature that would make it stand out
"What should I look at?"

Have a button:

Generate Insights

The application analyses the currently filtered data and produces 3–5 observations.

For example:

INSIGHT ENGINE

with the previous period.

of total revenue.

◆ OPPORTUNITY
Region C has the highest growth
